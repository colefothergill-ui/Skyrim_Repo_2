# Enemy Encounter Tables by Hold

This file provides Fate Core appropriate enemy stat blocks organized by Hold and difficulty tier.

## Using Encounter Tables

1. Determine PC power level (based on current act or refresh)
2. Select appropriate difficulty tier
3. Choose enemies from the Hold section matching current location
4. Adjust aspects and skills based on narrative context

## Difficulty Tiers

- **Mook (0 stress boxes)**: Taken out on any hit, groups of 3-6
- **Minor (2 stress boxes)**: Standard opposition, 1-2 at a time
- **Significant (3-4 stress boxes)**: Named enemies, lieutenants
- **Major (4+ stress boxes, consequences)**: Boss-level threats

## Creating Custom Encounters

### Quick Enemy Builder

**Step 1**: Choose power level (Mook/Minor/Significant/Major)  
**Step 2**: Assign stress boxes and consequences accordingly  
**Step 3**: Create High Concept matching role and location  
**Step 4**: Add Trouble aspect that creates vulnerability  
**Step 5**: Assign 2-4 skills at appropriate levels  
**Step 6**: Add 1-3 additional aspects for flavor and tactics

### Skill Caps by Tier
- **Mook**: Top skill +2, only 2 skills
- **Minor**: Top skill +2-3, 3-5 skills
- **Significant**: Top skill +3-4, 4-6 skills  
- **Major**: Top skill +4-5, 6+ skills, stunts optional

### Adding Stunts
Only Significant and Major enemies should have stunts. Keep them simple:
- "+2 to [skill] when [circumstance]"
- "Once per scene, [special ability]"
- "[Always/Never] [specific effect]"

## Encounter Composition Guidelines

### Solo Enemy
Use Significant or Major tier, or multiple Minors

### Small Group (2-4)
- 1 Significant + 2-3 Mooks, or
- 2-3 Minors, or  
- 4-6 Mooks

### Large Group (5+)
- 1 Major + 1-2 Significants + Mooks, or
- 1 Significant + 4-8 Mooks
