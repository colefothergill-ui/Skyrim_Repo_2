# Loot & Rewards System — Fate Core Style

In Fate Core, "loot" is less about +1 swords and more about **aspects**, **extras**, and **story permissions**. This document provides a Fate-appropriate reward system for Skyrim.

---

## Core Loot Philosophy

### What Loot Does in Fate Core:
1. **Creates new aspects** for invokes and compels
2. **Grants story permissions** (access to places, people, or knowledge)
3. **Provides mechanical extras** (minor bonuses or abilities)
4. **Advances character narrative** (ties to backstory or goals)

### What Loot Doesn't Do:
- Replace skill investment
- Make characters objectively "better"
- Stack numerical bonuses indefinitely

## Special Loot Categories

### Daedric Artifacts (Major, -1 to -2 Refresh)

All Daedric artifacts come with **powerful benefits and dangerous compels**:

- **Mehrunes' Razor** — Instant kill on successful Fight roll (once per session); "Mehrunes Watches"
- **Wabbajack** — Unpredictable effects (GM chooses); "Madness Incarnate"
- **Azura's Star** — Reusable soul gem for enchanting; "Azura's Chosen" (Daedric politics)
- **Spellbreaker** — +3 to Defend vs magic; "Peryite's Champion" (disease and plague drawn to you)

**GM Note**: Daedric artifacts should change the story. Award sparingly and with consequences.

## Wealth & Resources

### Gold as Narrative Resource
Fate Core doesn't track every septim. Use wealth brackets:

- **Penniless**: Can't afford lodging or supplies without help
- **Modest (50-200)**: Basic needs met; common gear accessible
- **Comfortable (200-1000)**: Fine lodging, quality gear, small bribes
- **Wealthy (1000-5000)**: Influence through gold; hire mercenaries
- **Rich (5000+)**: Political power; fund operations

### Using Wealth
Create advantages with Resources skill: "Well-Equipped," "Bribed the Guard," "Hired Expert Tracker"

## Loot by Victory Type

### Minor Victory (Ordinary Combat)
- 1-2 common items or aspects
- Modest gold (50-150)
- Tradeable goods

### Significant Victory (Tough Fight, Side Quest)
- 1 uncommon item or aspect
- 2-3 common items
- Comfortable gold (200-500)
- Possible permission

### Major Victory (Boss, Main Quest)
- 1 rare item or extra
- 1-2 uncommon items
- Wealthy gold (500-2000)
- Major permission or aspect

### Campaign Milestone
- Potential Daedric artifact or major extra
- Major permission (political position, faction leadership)
- Campaign-changing aspect

## Creating Custom Loot

### Aspect-Based Loot
**Template**: "[Descriptor] [Item Type]"  
**Effect**: 1-2 free invokes, thematic permission  
**Example**: "Jarl's Sealed Orders" — 2 free invokes on Deceive (official business)

### Permission-Based Loot
**Template**: "[Authority/Access] [Location/Group]"  
**Effect**: Story permission to enter, command, or request  
**Example**: "Dark Brotherhood Passwords" — Access to Sanctuaries

### Extra-Based Loot (Costs Refresh)
**Template**: "[Named Item]" (Extra, -X Refresh)  
**Effect**: Mechanical benefit + aspect  
**Compel**: Always include a compellable aspect  
**Example**: "Blade of Woe" (Extra, -1 Refresh) — +2 Stealth kills; aspect "Sithis Marks You"

## Loot Distribution Guidelines

### Session Rewards
Award 1-2 pieces of loot per session based on accomplishments.

### Milestone Rewards
At Fate milestones, allow:
- **Minor Milestone**: Swap an old loot aspect for a new one
- **Significant Milestone**: Upgrade common loot to uncommon, or gain new permission
- **Major Milestone**: Rare loot or extra becomes available

### Too Much Loot?
If PCs accumulate too many aspects:
- Invoke compels to lose items (stolen, broken, confiscated)
- Require choices: "You can only carry one legendary weapon"
- Time-limited aspects: "Potion of [Effect]" expires after use
