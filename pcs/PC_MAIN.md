## Notes
### Tagline
**"I Refuse to Be Eclipsed"**

### Backstory & Compel Fodder
Agran Moorcroft is talented, heroic, handsome — and now tainted. A blood-sucking fiend turned him and sent him off to war, eclipsed. He gallivants like a bard: performs extreme heroism worthy of ballads, even when it's tactically dumb.

His strain ("Sanguinar Vampirism") is new, potent, faster/stronger, and harder to hide. He's easily offended and still learning control. Only **Sybille Stentor** knows his secret and advises him; everyone else must see "mortal Agran."

**Reputation hooks:**
- Prodigy bard, early graduate from Bard's College
- Womanizer/performer with considerable charm
- Inspiring morale machine for Imperial troops
- Honorable soldier who cares about civilian protection

**Compel triggers:**
- Public attention drawing unwanted scrutiny
- Hunger/exposure risk in inopportune moments
- Overreacting to disrespect or challenges to his honor
- Choosing between power and secrecy
- Protecting civilians to look heroic (even when tactically unsound)
- Temptation to seduce/manipulate to solve problems
- Taking unnecessary risks to prove he's "not just a vampire"

### Relationships
**Hadvar of Solitude:**
- Knows Agran from Legion time routed through Solitude
- Saw his performances, trained with him, trusts him
- They are "shield-brothers" in the field
- Hadvar can vouch for him publicly, which is both a protection and a risk
- Hadvar does NOT know about the vampirism

**Sybille Stentor:**
- Court wizard of Solitude
- The ONLY person who knows Agran is a vampire
- Provides guidance and advice on managing the condition
- Acts as confidant and protector of his secret
